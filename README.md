# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis (New York)

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard analyzing retail sales performance across three coffee shop locations in **New York** from **January 2023 to June 2023**. This project transforms six months of transactional café data into clear, actionable visual insights to evaluate branch efficiency, product demand, and peak operating hours.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview
Managing multi-branch retail operations requires granular visibility into store traffic and product performance. The objective of this project is to:
* Compare revenue, order volume, and unit prices across **three New York locations** (Lower Manhattan, Hell's Kitchen, and Astoria).
* Analyze sales performance across distinct **product categories** (e.g., Coffee, Tea, Bakery, Drinking Chocolate).
* Track **monthly trends** and **hourly peak traffic** from January to June 2023 to support staffing and inventory decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, interactive dashboard design, and visual storytelling.
* **Power Query (M):** Data cleaning, date/time extraction, and ETL transformations.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom measures for Total Revenue, Total Quantity Sold, Average Order Value, and MoM Growth.

---

## 🔄 Data Preprocessing & Modeling
To prepare the transactional dataset for multi-dimensional time and branch analysis, the following steps were executed:
1. **Time Intelligence Extraction:** Extracted month names, days of the week, and transaction hours from raw timestamps using **Power Query** to enable peak-hour analysis.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Standardized product categories, store locations, and pricing formats to ensure calculation accuracy.
3. **KPI & Measure Development:** Built dynamic **DAX** measures to track revenue growth, quantities sold, and category contributions across individual branches.

---

## 📈 Key Analytical Highlights & Insights
* **Branch Comparison:** Evaluated revenue and transaction volume across all three New York branches to identify top-performing stores and regional demand differences.
* **Product Category Breakdown:** Analyzed unit prices against quantities sold to pinpoint core revenue generators versus high-margin add-ons.
* **Temporal & Peak-Hour Patterns:** Mapped monthly sales momentum and hourly customer rush periods to highlight peak business hours throughout the day.
